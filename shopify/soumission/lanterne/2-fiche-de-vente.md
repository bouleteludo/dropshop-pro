Textes à copier-coller dans le formulaire de soumission du Partner Dashboard.
Les explications sont en français, les textes à coller sont en anglais, car
le Theme Store est international. Les noms des champs peuvent varier
légèrement dans le formulaire.

**Nom du thème** — rempli automatiquement depuis le zip :

```
Lanterne
```

**Version :**

```
1.0.0
```

**Notes de version (release notes) :**

```
First release.
- Seasonal banner with a built-in calendar: schedule a banner for each celebration and it switches automatically on its yearly dates. Select a season in the theme editor to preview it.
- Scheduled announcements: each announcement can show only between two dates.
- Shipping deadline: one date in theme settings, shown in the announcement bar, on product pages and in the cart only during the days before it.
- Rising ember animation, turned off for visitors who prefer reduced motion.
- Storefront filtering, predictive search, accessible cart updates, rich product media, pickup availability, gift card recipients, selling plans, unit prices and Shop Pay Installments.
```

**Secteur d'activité (industrie)** — 2 maximum, à choisir dans la liste
proposée : prends ce qui se rapproche le plus de « Fêtes et déguisements »
(party, costumes) et, en second choix, « Maison et décoration ».

**Taille du catalogue** — choisis la catégorie « petit à moyen catalogue ».

**Description courte :**

```
A dark, elegant theme for costume and party shops, with a seasonal banner that switches automatically on the dates you choose.
```

**Description complète :**

```
Lanterne is lit like a lantern on a Halloween night: deep night tones, warm copper accents, rising embers and a dramatic banner that puts your costumes, masks and party decorations in the spotlight.

Party shops sell all year round. Lanterne's seasonal calendar lets you prepare a banner for each celebration — Halloween, carnival, New Year's Eve — and switches to the right one automatically on its dates. Schedule your announcements the same way, and show customers the real last day to order before the big night.
```

**Fonctions à cocher (features)** — coche celles qui existent dans la liste :

```
Seasonal banner with calendar, scheduled announcements, shipping deadline, animations, color swatches, product videos, 3D models, product tabs, shipping and delivery information, cross-selling (complementary products), product recommendations, predictive search, storefront filtering, multi-level menus, sticky header, country and language selectors, newsletter signup, high-resolution images.
```
