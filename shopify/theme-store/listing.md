Copy for the Partner Dashboard submission form. One listing page per style.
Review the wording before submitting.

## Theme

**Name:** Lanterne
**Version:** 1.0.0

**Short description**
A seasonal storefront for holiday, gift and party shops: schedule your
banners by date, show a real shipping deadline and guide shoppers to the right
gift.

**Release notes — 1.0.0**
- First release with five styles: Lanterne, Sapin, Printemps, Velours and
  Rivage.
- Seasonal banner with a built-in calendar: schedule a banner for each holiday
  and it switches automatically on its dates. Select a season in the theme
  editor to preview it.
- Scheduled announcements: each announcement can show only between two dates.
- Holiday shipping deadline: one date in theme settings, shown in the
  announcement bar, on product pages and in the cart only during the days
  before it.
- Gift guide section: budget cards that open the collection filtered by price.
- Gift message in the cart, saved with the order.
- Storefront filtering, predictive search, quick and accessible cart updates,
  rich product media, pickup availability, gift card recipients, selling
  plans, unit prices and Shop Pay Installments.

## Features to select

Seasonal banner with calendar, scheduled announcements, shipping deadline,
gift guide, gift message, color swatches, product videos, 3D models, product
tabs, shipping and delivery information, cross-selling (complementary
products), product recommendations, predictive search, storefront filtering,
multi-level menus, sticky header, animations, country and language
selectors, newsletter signup, high-resolution images.

## Styles

### Lanterne
- **Segment:** costumes and party supplies
- **Catalog size:** small to medium
- **Description:** A dark, elegant storefront lit like a lantern on a
  Halloween night. Rising ember effects, warm copper accents and a dramatic
  banner bring costumes, masks and party decorations to life. Schedule your
  Halloween banner ahead, then let the calendar switch to your next season.

### Sapin
- **Segment:** Christmas decorations and gifts
- **Catalog size:** medium
- **Description:** Deep fir green, festive red and golden light. Sapin turns
  your store into a snowy holiday village with a gentle snowfall effect, a
  gift guide by budget and a real shipping deadline so shoppers know when to
  order for delivery before Christmas.

### Printemps
- **Segment:** flowers and spring decorations
- **Catalog size:** small to medium
- **Description:** Ivory, moss green and antique gold. A bright, refined style
  with falling petals for florists, home decor and Easter gift shops, with
  elegant serif typography and soft, natural light.

### Velours
- **Segment:** jewelry and romantic gifts
- **Catalog size:** small
- **Description:** Deep red velvet, roses and candlelight. Velours puts
  jewelry and romantic gifts in an intimate setting, with gift messages in the
  cart and a shipping deadline for Valentine's Day orders.

### Rivage
- **Segment:** beach and summer accessories
- **Catalog size:** small to medium
- **Description:** Warm sand, deep teal and golden hour. Rivage brings the
  seaside to beachwear, accessories and summer gift shops, with sparkling
  light effects and a bold, sunny banner.
