**Segment:** costumes and party supplies
**Catalog size:** small to medium

## Short description

A dark, elegant theme for costume and party shops, with a seasonal banner
that switches automatically on the dates you choose.

## Description

Lanterne is lit like a lantern on a Halloween night: deep night tones, warm
copper accents, rising embers and a dramatic banner that puts your costumes,
masks and party decorations in the spotlight.

Party shops sell all year round. Lanterne's seasonal calendar lets you prepare
a banner for each celebration — Halloween, carnival, New Year's Eve — and
switches to the right one automatically on its dates. Schedule your
announcements the same way, and show customers the real last day to order
before the big night.

## Release notes — 1.0.0

- First release.
- Seasonal banner with a built-in calendar: schedule a banner for each
  celebration and it switches automatically on its yearly dates. Select a
  season in the theme editor to preview it.
- Scheduled announcements: each announcement can show only between two dates.
- Shipping deadline: one date in theme settings, shown in the announcement
  bar, on product pages and in the cart only during the days before it.
- Rising ember animation, turned off for visitors who prefer reduced motion.
- Storefront filtering, predictive search, quick and accessible cart updates,
  rich product media, pickup availability, gift card recipients, selling
  plans, unit prices and Shop Pay Installments.
