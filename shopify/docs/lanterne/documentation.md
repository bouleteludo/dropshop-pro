# Lanterne theme documentation

Lanterne is a Shopify theme for costume and party shops. Its seasonal
calendar lets you plan a banner for every celebration of the year.

> Before editing code, duplicate your theme (Online Store > Themes > … >
> Duplicate). Code edits are not covered by support; for custom work, hire a
> Shopify Partner.

## Getting started

1. Install Lanterne from the Shopify Theme Store.
2. Online Store > Themes > Customize.
3. Upload your logo (Theme settings > Logo). Without a logo, your store name
   is shown.
4. Set up your menus: the header uses **Main menu** and the footer uses
   **Footer** (Online Store > Navigation).
5. Add your policies (Settings > Policies). Links appear in the footer.

## Seasonal banner and calendar

The **Seasonal banner** section shows a large image with your message.

- **Default banner:** the content shown when no season is active.
- **Seasons:** add a *Season* block for each holiday and set its start and
  end dates. Between these dates, the season replaces the default banner
  automatically. Dates repeat every year, and a season can span the new year
  (for example December 26 to January 6). When two seasons overlap, the first
  one in the list wins.
- **Preview:** in the theme editor, select a season to preview it.
- **Images:** upload your own image (1920 x 1080px recommended), or use the
  built-in Halloween image. Set a focal point in Content > Files to control
  cropping.
- **Animation:** rising embers. It is turned off automatically for visitors who
  prefer reduced motion.

Dates use your store's time zone. Pages can be cached for a short time, so a
change of season can take a few minutes to appear.

## Scheduled announcements

In the **Announcement bar**, open an announcement and turn on **Show only
between two dates**. Outside these dates, the announcement is hidden.

## Shipping deadline

Theme settings > **Holiday shipping deadline**:

1. Turn on **Show the shipping deadline**.
2. Set the deadline month and day, and how many days before the date the
   message appears.
3. Write the message. `[date]` is replaced with the formatted date, for example
   "Order by October 25 for delivery in time for Halloween."

The message appears in the announcement bar (when **Show holiday shipping
deadline** is on), on product pages (with the **Holiday shipping deadline**
block) and in the cart. It disappears after the deadline. Only use a date you
can honor.

## Product pages

The **Product information** section is made of blocks you can reorder: title,
vendor, price (with Shop Pay Installments), variant picker (buttons or
dropdown, with color swatches), quantity, buy buttons (with dynamic checkout
buttons and the gift card recipient form), holiday shipping deadline,
description, collapsible tabs, complementary products, custom Liquid and app
blocks.

Complementary and related products are set in the **Search & Discovery** app.

## Other sections

Highlights, Featured product, Featured collection, Collection list, Image with
text, Rich text, Newsletter, Contact form and Custom Liquid.

## FAQ

**The season didn't change on the expected day.**
Check the dates of the season block and your store's time zone (Settings >
General). Seasons are checked from top to bottom; move the season up if two
overlap.

**How do I translate the theme?**
French and English are included. Use Online Store > Themes > … > Edit
default theme content, or a translation app.
